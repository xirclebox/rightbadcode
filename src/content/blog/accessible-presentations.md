Let's talk about creating accessible slides for conferences.

Read your comments out loud

Use colors that pass A11y for content

Be mindful of flickering animations

Use large font sizes

Use fonts with heavy strokes

Keep slide content brief

Be mindful of slide UX. If users are on laptops, provide links. If users are mobile, use QR codes.

Don't assume everyone can hear you when you don't use the mic.

Use captions for videos

Use the whole screen

When talking about perception, show the blue-black / white-gold dress.
