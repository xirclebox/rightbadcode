Suppose you live in a world where doorknobs are a nice-to-have. And in this world, you live in a house without any other means of opening your doors from the outside, and the only way to get into your home is through an open window using a stepladder. Though you may be used to climbing through your window, it’s still a struggle and a hassle to have to do so, especially when your neighbor is able to walk through their front door with ease.
