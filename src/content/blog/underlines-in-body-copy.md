underscore, lodash, or underline

Avoid design decisions with content that can make the user think there's something wrong. For example, using the underline to highlight text makes the text look like a link. When the user tries to click on it, they may think something is wrong with the app or that they've done something wrong.

Underscoring text was a method of highlighting text in the early days due to the lack of ability to use bold words while using a typewriter.

In web pages, hyperlinks are usually underlined to show they are links, not for emphasis. But can the underline be seen as emphasizing since it's meant to make the text link stand out from the rest of the copy?
