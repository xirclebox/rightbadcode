A question I get often is "what specific disabilities should we be concerned with?"

This is not the correct approach to addressing accessibility issues with your products and services.

Disabilities are not a linear gradient or binary, instead, they are more like a gradient mesh forming a beautiful spectrum that's made up of all of us.

By trying to solve for one you may still exclude many of the very people you think you're helping. We have the tools available to all involved in the product lifecycle to produce and launch digital products with robust accessibility support. These same tools can be used to address legacy decisions as well.
