Darryl Zanuck said something wild back in the late 40s. I’m paraphrasing, but he basically said TV was a fad and that people would grow bored with it after a while. Then there was Clifford Stoll, who in the late 90s thought the internet would crash and burn before 2000. There was also this 10-year-old kid named Homer Gaines who, back in the 80s, told his parents that only rich people would have computers because they cost too much. Boy, were they wrong!

Fast forward to today, there’s no shortage of naysayers regarding the use of modern AI. There’s a lot of doubt, fear, and optimism around what this tech can do or how it will be used. Regardless of your views, one thing’s for sure… barring some enormous seismic shift, this new generation of AI is here to stay for the foreseeable future.

You’d be hard-pressed to find a sector that’s not trying to include AI to some degree. As a result, at the consumer level, the tools people will need to use to interact with AI will need to be accessible.
